About Diana

A nurse's eye for care, brought to your feet

I spent years working as a Registered Nurse across orthopaedics, theatres and elderly care, including time in care homes, looking after people who couldn't always speak up for themselves.

Feet are often the last thing anyone thinks about. But for older people, or anyone with reduced mobility or diabetes, they matter more than most people realise. A missed infection, an ingrown nail or a corn left untreated can turn into a much bigger problem: a fall, a hospital admission, weeks of pain that didn't need to happen.

That's what led me into specialist foot health care. I trained as a Foot Health Practitioner (MCFHP MAFHP) through The SMAE Institute, and now I bring that same nursing attention (careful, unhurried and dignified) straight to your home.

"You shouldn't have to struggle to get your feet looked after properly. I come to you instead."

Diana Scuckaite, MCFHP MAFHP

Some people can't reach their own feet anymore. Others are managing diabetes, or would just rather have someone qualified come to them. Either way, I treat every appointment the way I'd want my own family looked after.

What's Included

One visit, everything your feet need

There's no price list to work through item by item. Every home visit is a full assessment and treatment, tailored to what your feet need on the day.

Full foot assessment

Checking the health of your skin, nails and circulation before any treatment starts, so small problems get caught before they become big ones.

Nail cutting

Careful trimming and filing for nails that are thick, curved or hard to reach, the kind that cause pressure, pain and ingrown edges if left alone.

Fungal nail care

Treatment and ongoing management for nails that have gone thickened, discoloured or brittle, so they don't spread or get worse.

Diabetic foot care

Extra checks on circulation and skin, because for diabetic feet a small cut or blister can turn serious fast. Early warning signs matter.

Corn removal

Safe, professional removal of hardened skin, not a blade or a chemist's plaster you shouldn't be using on your own feet.

Heel care

Treatment for dry, cracked or hard skin on the heels, before it splits and starts to hurt.

Verruca treatment

Careful treatment and monitoring for verrucas, so they're less likely to spread or cause pain when walking.

FAQ

Questions people ask before booking

Is this the same as NHS podiatry?

Not quite. NHS podiatry is usually reserved for people at the most serious foot health risk, and waiting lists can be long. Private foot health care like Diana's fills the gap (routine nail care, corns, verrucas, fungal nails and general foot health) without the wait, and in your own home.

Is it safe for diabetic feet?

Yes. Diabetic feet need extra care because minor issues can turn serious quickly, and that's exactly what Diana's training covers. She checks your circulation and skin carefully at every visit, and will always let you know if something needs a doctor's attention.

What happens at a first visit?

Diana starts with a full assessment of your feet, asks about your general health and any conditions like diabetes, then carries out your treatment. The whole appointment takes around 45 minutes, and she'll talk you through what she's doing as she goes.

How does payment work?

It's £40 for the full visit, paid by cash or bank transfer on the day. If you're more than 5 miles outside Diana's usual area, there's a small £10 travel charge, which she'll always confirm with you before you book.

Does she bring her own equipment?

Yes, Diana brings a full set of her own sterilised, professional-grade instruments to every visit. Nothing is borrowed and nothing is shared between clients.

Is Diana DBS checked?

Yes. Diana is DBS checked, fully insured and holds her MCFHP MAFHP qualification through The SMAE Institute, so you can feel confident having her visit your home.

What areas are covered?

Diana covers Wembley Park, Wembley, North Wembley, Preston, Kingsbury, Neasden, Alperton, Sudbury, Kenton, Harlesden, Willesden and Park Royal, plus parts of Edgware and Harrow, as well as care homes, hospitals and private hospitals. Just outside that list? Message your postcode and she'll let you know.
